Common questions

 

Who do you work with?

Visa holders, green card holders, students on OPT or CPT, families, freelancers, founders, small businesses and larger companies with multi-state needs.

Do I need to file an FBAR?

Generally yes, if the combined highest balance of your foreign accounts went over $10,000 at any point in the year. This includes savings and NRE or NRO accounts.

Can I file as a resident while on an H-1B?

It depends on how many days you were in the US and your visa history. We confirm your status before preparing anything.

What if I missed prior years?

We can prepare late returns and related filings such as FBAR for earlier years. Start with your most recent year and we’ll work backward.

How is my data protected?

Documents are encrypted in transit and at rest, and access to your account requires identity verification.
